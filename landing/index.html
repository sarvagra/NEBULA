Built for being together

One room. Everyone on the same second.

Playback is host-led and server-stamped, so a pause, a seek or a video switch lands on every phone at once — no “wait, go back”.

Synced watch parties

Host leads; everyone follows in real time. Play, pause and seek are broadcast with a server timestamp, so drift stays invisible.

Any link you can paste

YouTube, Vimeo, Prime Video or a direct video file. Drop a link and the room switches to it for everybody.

A queue, not a guess

Line up what plays next from any source. Members vote to skip; the host can advance the line.

Chat that behaves

Sent, delivered and seen ticks. Edit, unsend, reply to a specific message, pile up to five photos, and hide anything behind a spoiler.

Friends first

See who is online right now, jump into their room, or invite them to yours. Friend requests stay in sync everywhere they appear.

Profiles with taste

Banner, persona tags and top content drawn from the rooms you actually spend time in. Yours to keep public or private, per section.

A look inside

Dark by default, because most of this happens at night. Everything you touch sits within thumb reach.

Questions

Good to know.

Is Nebula free?

Yes. During the beta every feature is unlocked for every account — rooms, queues, custom profiles and all chat features.

Do I need a Google account?

Sign-in uses Google, and you can continue with an email one-time code if you prefer. One account holds one identity across devices.

Can two people use the same account in a room?

No. An account may be signed in on up to two devices, but only one of them can be inside a watch room at a time. The second attempt is declined with a clear message.

How many people fit in a room?

Hosts choose a capacity of 4, 8, 12, 16, 20 or 24 seats when creating a room, and can change it later.

What happens to my messages?

They are stored on Nebula’s servers so they arrive on every device, and they stay until you unsend them or delete the conversation. See the in-app privacy policy for the retention details.

Will the app update itself?

Not yet. Each release is a manual install over the previous one. Your data is safe — the signature stays the same, so Android treats it as an update.
